# Galaxy Gridiron on iPhone or iPad (about 1 minute)

1. Open the link you were sent in **Safari**. Chrome on iPhone also works on iOS 16.4 and later, but Safari is simplest. On iPad (including iPadOS 13+), use Safari too.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow). On iPhone it's at the bottom of the screen; on iPad it's in the Safari toolbar at the top.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. Open **Galaxy Gridiron** from the new icon on your Home Screen. Don't use the Safari tab.
5. Skip or step through the short tour, then type **your own Sleeper username** (not your display name) and tap **Launch 🚀**. In the Sleeper app, your username is on the page you get by tapping your profile icon in the bottom-left corner.

Good to know
- It's read-only. There's no password, and it can't change anything in your leagues.
- Your saved picks live on this device, inside the Home Screen app. If you delete the icon, the picks go with it,
  so use Settings › Picks data › Export backup first. Picks opened in a normal Safari tab may be cleared by iOS or iPadOS.
- When a new version is out, a "↻ Reload to update" banner appears. Tap it.
